## Nearby Attractions

### Ella Rock Hike
**Distance from Cottage**: 1.5 km to trailhead
**Difficulty**: Moderate (some steep sections)
**Duration**: 4-5 hours round trip
**Best Time**: Start at 5:30 AM for sunrise views
**Fitness Level**: Good fitness required

**Trail Details**:
- Spectacular 360° panoramic views from summit
- Trail passes through tea plantations and forest
- Some steep rocky sections near the summit
- Well-marked path but can be slippery when wet

**What to Bring**:
- At least 1.5 liters of water per person
- Sunscreen and hat
- Comfortable hiking shoes (not sandals!)
- Light snacks or packed breakfast
- Camera for amazing photos
- Light rain jacket (weather changes quickly)

**Tips from Nalaka**:
- Start early to avoid midday heat and clouds
- The sunrise from the summit is unforgettable
- Don't rely on phone GPS - get our printed map
- The last section is steep but worth it!
- Watch for leeches during monsoon season

**Nalaka provides**: Free detailed maps and hiking guidance for all guests!

### Nine Arch Bridge
**Distance from Cottage**: 2 km
**How to Get There**: 25-minute walk or 5-minute tuk-tuk
**Best Time**: 6:00 AM for sunrise, 3:30 PM for train photo

**Details**:
- Iconic colonial-era railway bridge
- Train passes approximately every 2-3 hours
- Beautiful through the tea plantations
- Great photo opportunity

### Little Adam's Peak
**Distance from Cottage**: 3 km
**Difficulty**: Easy to Moderate (family-friendly)
**Duration**: 2-3 hours round trip
**Best Time**: Early morning or late afternoon
**Suitable For**: Families with children, beginners, all fitness levels

**Trail Details**:
- Easier alternative to Ella Rock
- Well-maintained path through tea plantations
- Stunning views of Ella Gap valley
- Multiple viewpoints along the way
- Shaded sections for rest

**What to Bring**:
- Water (1 liter per person)
- Sunscreen
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Camera

**Family Tips**:
- Great for kids aged 5 and up
- Takes about 45 minutes to reach summit
- Plenty of photo opportunities
- Can combine with Nine Arch Bridge visit
- Tea shop at the base for refreshments

### Ravana Falls
**Distance from Cottage**: 5 km
**How to Get There**: Tuk-tuk ($5) or bus
**Duration**: 1-2 hours

**Details**:
- 25-meter waterfall
- Swimming pool at base (during dry season)
- Cave nearby with Buddhist shrine
- Good for afternoon trip

### Lipton's Seat
**Distance from Cottage**: 20 km
**Duration**: Half-day trip
**Best Time**: Before 10 AM (for clear views)

**Details**:
- Panoramic viewpoint over tea country
- Named after Sir Thomas Lipton
- Tuk-tuk tour recommended ($25-30)
- Stop at tea factory on the way
