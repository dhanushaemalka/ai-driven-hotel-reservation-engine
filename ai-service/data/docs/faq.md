## Food & Dining

### Where can I get authentic Sri Lankan food?

- Cooking class with Renu - learn to cook traditional dishes
- Dinner can be arranged with advance notice

**Nearby restaurants:**
- **Ella Village Restaurant** (500m) - Best rice & curry, kottu
- **Matey Hut** (1km) - Seafood and local dishes
- **Local street stalls** - Authentic, budget-friendly

### What is Sri Lankan food like?
Sri Lankan cuisine features:
- **Rice and curry** - Multiple curries with rice (our specialty!)
- **Hoppers** - Bowl-shaped pancakes, often with egg
- **Kottu** - Chopped roti with vegetables and meat
- **String hoppers** - Steamed rice noodles
- **Sambol** - Spicy coconut condiment

Renu's famous **Jackfruit Curry** is a must-try!

### Do you cater to dietary restrictions?
Yes! We can accommodate:
- Vegetarian/Vegan options
- Gluten-free (rice-based dishes)
- Mild spice levels
- Allergies (please inform us in advance)

## Practical Information

### How do I get to Cloudy Hill Cottage?
**From Colombo:**
- Train to Ella (scenic 6-7 hour journey) - Highly recommended!
- Bus (5-6 hours)
- Private car/taxi ($80-100)

**From Kandy:**
- Train (scenic route, 6 hours)
- Bus (4-5 hours)

We can arrange **airport/station pickup** - just ask!

### Is Ella safe?
Yes, Ella is very safe for tourists. It's a small, friendly town with low crime. The locals are welcoming and helpful.

### What's the weather like?
Ella has pleasant mountain weather:
- **Temperature**: 15-25°C year-round
- **Best weather**: January-March (dry season)
- **Monsoon**: May-September (occasional rain, misty, beautiful)
- **Pack**: Light layers, rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes

### Do I need to book in advance?
**Peak season (Dec-Apr):** Yes, book at least 2-3 weeks ahead
**Low season (May-Sep):** Walk-ins usually available, but booking recommended
